Background

I test products before writing about them. That sounds obvious but it is not standard practice. Most product content is rewritten spec sheets or affiliate-optimized lists assembled without opening a single box.

TopProduct exists because I got tired of buying things based on reviews written by people who had never touched them. Every pick here has been through real use - not a press preview, not a sponsored send.

How I Approach Product Reviews & Buying Guides
1
Start with what the evidence shows
Before applying any strategy, understand what the research actually supports - not what popular sources claim.
2
Layer complexity deliberately
Add one new layer at a time. Understand each piece before moving to the next.
3
Run the realistic numbers
Most projections skip the hard parts: time, cost, and real-world friction. Do the honest math first.
4
Structure beats hustle
How you organize your approach matters as much as what you do. Systems compound over time.
5
Accept the realistic timeline
Meaningful results take time. Those who succeed are the ones who stopped looking for shortcuts.
