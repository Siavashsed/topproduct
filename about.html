Why this site exists

I test products before writing about them. That sounds obvious but it is not standard practice. Most product content is rewritten spec sheets or affiliate-optimized lists assembled without opening a single box.

TopProduct exists because I got tired of buying things based on reviews written by people who had never touched them. Every pick here has been through real use - not a press preview, not a sponsored send.
