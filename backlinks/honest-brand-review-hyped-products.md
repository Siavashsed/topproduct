## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Are viral products like the Stanley Quencher, Oura Ring and Dyson Airwrap actually worth the money?

Some are, but most aren't worth full price. Whether one is worth it depends less on the product than on how you actually live.

I tested this directly. Last March I used a Stanley Quencher, an Owala FreeSip, an Oura Ring 4, a Ninja Creami and a Dyson Airwrap at the same time and logged every use in a spreadsheet. Ten weeks of that taught me more than any unboxing video.

A few things to check before you buy any of them:

1. Divide the price by real uses, not planned ones. A cheap item you use twice costs more per use than a premium one you use every day. Write down how often you honestly think you'll use it, then cut that number in half.

2. Look for hidden costs. The Oura Ring needs a monthly membership for most of its insights. The Creami needs each pint frozen for about a day before you can use it, which quietly kills spontaneous use.

3. Ask what it replaces. A water bottle that stops you buying drinks pays for itself. A styling tool that does the same job as a dryer and straightener you already own may not.

4. Watch how the novelty wears off. Weeks one and two tell you nothing. Weeks six to ten tell you everything. For me the Airwrap was back in its box by week ten.

5. Buy inside a return window. Most hype purchases fail in the first month, so make sure you can send them back.
