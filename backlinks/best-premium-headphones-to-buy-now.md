**Answer this question:** What are the best premium headphones to buy if I'm willing to spend over $400?

Short answer: for most people spending $400 or more, get the Sony WH-1000XM6. Buy something else only if you have a specific reason to.

I'm Jamie Cole. I review consumer products, and I tested this the uncomfortable way. In March I flew JFK to LAX with three premium pairs in my carry-on, swapped them every 90 minutes and noted what I noticed on a napkin. Long sessions matter because most of what separates headphones at this price shows up after the first hour, not in a store demo.

Here's what I learned:

1. Noise cancelling is close at the top. The Bose blocked low engine drone a little better than the Sony. If cancellation is the one thing you care about, especially if you fly often, that edge is real, but it's small.

2. Comfort over hours is the real difference. The Sony was the only pair I didn't want to take off when the timer went. Clamp force, heat around the ears and weight on top of your head are hard to judge in ten minutes and impossible to ignore in hour four.

3. Know your specific reason before you buy. Good reasons to pick something other than the Sony: you want the strongest possible cancellation, your glasses sit better under a different pad shape, or you're deep in one phone ecosystem and value seamless device switching.

4. Try them for longer than you think you need to. If a store lets you, wear a pair for 30 minutes, not three. Check the return window and actually use it.
